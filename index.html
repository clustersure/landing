NET-01 · Default-deny network policyHigh

Observed

6 of 9 application namespaces have no NetworkPolicy. Any pod can reach any other pod, including the payments service.

Why it matters

One compromised workload can move laterally across the cluster. Auditors treat this as a segmentation gap.

Fix

# apply per namespace, then allow known flows
apiVersion: networking.k8s.io/v1
kind: NetworkPolicy
metadata:
  name: default-deny
  namespace: payments
spec:
  podSelector: {}
  policyTypes: [Ingress, Egress]

Maps to

CIS Kubernetes 5.3.2 · ISO 27001:2022 A.8.22

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What access do you need?

A read-only IAM role in each AWS account in scope, created from a Terraform or CloudFormation template we provide, plus read-only cluster access mapped to that role. We never need write access to assess, and access is revoked at the end of the engagement.

Do you install anything in our clusters?

Not by default. Most checks run against the Kubernetes and AWS APIs from outside the cluster. If a node-level check such as kube-bench is useful, we agree it with you first and remove it afterwards.

We are a health tech company. Can you map findings to the DSPT?

Yes. Findings can be mapped to the NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit, Cyber Essentials and ISO 27001, which together cover most of what NHS customers ask health tech suppliers for. Tell us which frameworks matter on the scoping call.

How is the price set?

By scope: the number of clusters and AWS accounts, and whether pipeline review is included. You get a fixed price in writing before any work starts, and it does not change unless you change the scope.

What happens to our data?

Scan output and evidence are stored encrypted, kept only for the engagement and an agreed retention period, then deleted. We are happy to sign your NDA or work under your data processing terms.

We are not on EKS. Can you still help?

The posture review is built for EKS, but IAM, pipeline security and evidence automation apply to any AWS estate. Ask on the call and we will tell you plainly whether it is a good fit.

Can you fix what you find?

Yes. A remediation sprint delivers the fixes as pull requests to your repositories, so your team reviews and owns every change.
